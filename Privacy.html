Privacy Policy for AccessibleChessOne

Last updated: November 13, 2026

AccessibleChessOne respects your privacy.

Data collection

AccessibleChessOne does not use third-party advertising and does not track you across apps or websites.

The app may store the following information locally on your device or through the optional services you select to provide app features:

  • App settings and preferences
  • Saved game data
  • Move logs and PGN text generated by the user
  • Game Center match state when you choose to use Game Center
  • Lichess access token when you choose to sign in to Lichess or paste a personal API token
  • FICS guest session state while connected
  • Widget and Live Activity state needed to show current game information
  • iMessage game state needed to continue turn-based games on supported platforms

How data is used

This information is used only to provide core app features such as:

  • Restoring saved games
  • Remembering accessibility and gameplay settings
  • Submitting moves to selected online services
  • Displaying the current game in widgets or Live Activities
  • Continuing iMessage games
  • Supporting optional Game Center gameplay

Online services

If you choose to use Lichess, the app communicates with Lichess.org using Lichess APIs. Lichess is authoritative for Lichess games, challenges, chat, puzzles, and account data.

If you choose to use FICS, the app connects to the official Free Internet Chess Server as a guest. FICS uses plaintext TCP; registered-account login is not exposed in AccessibleChessOne.

Tracking

AccessibleChessOne does not track users.

Third parties

AccessibleChessOne uses Apple platform services such as Game Center, widgets, Live Activities, Messages, and Keychain when you choose to use those features. Their operation is subject to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can:

  • Delete saved games from within the app
  • Disable spoken moves, sound effects, haptics, widgets, or Live Activities where supported
  • Sign out of Lichess to remove the stored Lichess token from Keychain
  • Stop using Game Center, Lichess, FICS, or iMessage gameplay features at any time

Retention and recovery

Saved games and preferences remain on your device until removed. To protect against data loss, the app may retain local recovery copies of damaged or older saved-game data. Deleting a game from the active saved-game list does not necessarily remove these recovery copies. They are not automatically restored to your saved-game list. Online services control retention of data sent to them; signing out of the app does not delete your account or past games on those services.

Camera and augmented reality

If you choose AR View on a supported device, the app uses the camera and motion tracking to place the chessboard on a detected surface. You can use the standard board without enabling AR and manage camera permission in device Settings.

Diagnostics

The app uses Apple diagnostic facilities, including local performance and error logging. Apple may provide crash and performance reports according to your device and analytics-sharing settings.

Support requests

If you email support, Anthony Ure receives your email address and the information you choose to send, and uses it to respond to your request. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: anthonyure@gmail.com
