AccessibleChessOne Support

Thank you for using AccessibleChessOne.

Support contact: anthonyure@gmail.com

What the app supports:

  • Accessible chess gameplay
  • VoiceOver-first board navigation
  • Spoken moves and optional compact notation
  • Human and computer play
  • Saved games and move review
  • Game Center support
  • Lichess online play
  • FICS guest online play
  • Widgets and Live Activity support where available
  • iMessage chess games on supported platforms

If you need help, please include:

  • Your device model (iPhone, iPad, Mac, or Apple Watch)
  • Your operating system version
  • Whether you installed the app through TestFlight or the App Store
  • The app version and build number
  • A short description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the issue
  • Whether VoiceOver, a Braille display, larger text, or another accessibility setting was enabled

Do not include passwords, access tokens, or private conversations in your support request.

Common help topics

VoiceOver and board navigation

Explore the board square by square. The app announces each square and piece, and exposes accessible actions for legal moves.

Spoken moves

Spoken move announcements can be enabled or disabled in Settings. Voice choices and compact move notation can also be configured.

Online play

Lichess and FICS are optional. Lichess requires sign-in or an API token. FICS connects as a guest because the official FICS port is plaintext TCP.

Saved games and move log

AccessibleChessOne records moves for games played in the app, including supported online games, so the move log and PGN export remain available.

Mac Catalyst behavior

The Mac app is built from the same AccessibleChessOne codebase and uses Mac-compatible icons, menus, windows, and Catalyst controls.
